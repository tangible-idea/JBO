The technology

What is JEV?

JEV is an AI model from TypeSafe that understands text and makes focused decisions. Give it information and a question; it returns a choice, a score, or a yes/no probability. An app uses those answers to take the next step.1

ChooseWhich folder fits this page?
CheckDoes this entry describe work experience?
RateHow closely does this experience match the role?

The idea: break a bigger task into small, clear questions, then combine the answers. Probabilities help flag uncertain decisions.2

Try it on your idea

What do you repeatedly choose, check, or compare? Turn one of those tasks into a question.

01 / Tidymark

A place for
every bookmark.

A helper for organizing saved webpages

Tidymark gives JEV a page’s title, URL, and description and asks which existing bookmark folder fits best.

It also sorts accumulated bookmarks in batches, flagging uncertain items for review.

In practice

“Kyoto café guide”
→ Suggest “Travel / Japan”
→ Confirm and save

The benefit: Less time choosing folders; saved links become easier to find.

02 / ResumeDot

A clearer view
of each résumé.

A helper for organizing and comparing résumés

ResumeDot uses JEV to select names, contact details, employers, and roles, and distinguish work experience from education.

It rates experience, skills, qualifications, and overall fit against a job description. The app combines these ratings into a weighted score out of 100.

In practice

“Customer support experience required”
→ Assess relevant résumé evidence
→ Show individual and overall scores

The benefit: Compare résumés using consistent criteria and spot areas to explore further.
