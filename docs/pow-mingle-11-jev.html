How JEV works

JEV is an AI model from TypeSafe. It understands text and answers focused questions with choices, probabilities, or scores that an app can use.1

You define the question and possible answers. The app combines small judgments into a useful workflow; probabilities help flag uncertainty.

When JEV is not a good fit

  • Writing replies or code, or explaining itself1
  • Images, audio, or video (text only, for now)1
  • Open-ended questions with no set answers
  • Exact rules, math, or lookups (use plain code)
  • High-stakes calls with no human review

JEV(Decisions API) in practice

Pow Mingle #11 · Two projects using small AI decisions

1. Tidymark

Organizing bookmarks

tidy.tmtt.link

Tidymark gives JEV a page’s title, URL, and description, and asks which existing bookmark folder fits best.

It also sorts accumulated bookmarks in batches, flagging uncertain items for review.

Example

“Seongsu pop-ups this weekend”
→ Weekend plans 94% · saved
“Notion vs. Obsidian review”
→ Tools 52% / Reading 45% · asks you

Why it helps: Less time choosing folders, and saved links become easier to find.

2. ResumeDot

Scoring resumes against a job post

resumedot.com

Paste a job post from LinkedIn, drop in resumes, and see who fits the role best.

Why it helps: It is fast. 100 resumes are scored and ranked in seconds.
