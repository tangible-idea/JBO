About Pow Mingle

Our group is open to everyone interested in making interesting things, whether or not you work in tech.

AI tools and insights arrive every day. It can be hard to find time to understand them, let alone try them. Each month, we explore one new AI technology and apply it to our own ideas.

Bring your curiosity, an everyday frustration, or something you want to make. You do not need a technical background or a finished project.

Why meet at a restaurant?

Sharing a meal gives us a relaxed way to meet. Conversation can begin with what we do, what interests us, or what we are trying to figure out.

A restaurant makes room for people with different backgrounds to talk at the same table. We can ask basic questions, exchange unfinished ideas, and learn from one another over food.

The setting is part of the idea: make it easier to join the conversation, get to know someone, and find something worth trying together.

What to try tonight

How JEV works

JEV is an AI model from TypeSafe. It understands text and answers focused questions with choices, probabilities, or scores that an app can use.1

You define the question and possible answers. The app combines small judgments into a useful workflow; probabilities help flag uncertainty.

On the back, see how the same idea helps organize bookmarks and compare résumés.

Start with one question

“Which one fits?”, “Does this count?”, or “How well does it match?”

JEV in practice

Pow Mingle #11 · Two projects using small AI decisions

1. Tidymark

Organizing bookmarks

tidy.tmtt.link

Tidymark gives JEV a page’s title, URL, and description, and asks which existing bookmark folder fits best.

It also sorts accumulated bookmarks in batches, flagging uncertain items for review.

Example

“Kyoto cafés” → “Travel / Japan”
Confirm the suggestion and save.

Why it helps: Less time choosing folders, and saved links become easier to find.

The small question:
Which existing folder is the best fit for this page?

2. ResumeDot

Organizing and comparing résumés

resumedot.com

JEV helps select names, contact details, employers, and roles, and separate work experience from education.

The app combines JEV’s ratings for experience, skills, qualifications, and overall fit into a weighted score out of 100.

Example

A customer support role
→ Compare résumé evidence and scores

Why it helps: Compare résumés using consistent criteria and find areas to explore further.

The small questions:
Is this work experience? How well does the evidence match the role?
